Encoded Therapeutics Raises $275 Million, Could Double Its Morrisville Workforce

October 2026 · Morrisville, NC

Biotechnology company Encoded Therapeutics has secured $275 million in new financing, potentially paving the way for a significant expansion of its operations in Morrisville, North Carolina.

The California-based company develops gene therapies for serious neurological disorders, including Dravet syndrome, a rare form of epilepsy.

The funding, announced on September 9, 2026, will support clinical trials and the expansion of the company's pharmaceutical manufacturing capabilities.

According to a September 11 report by Triangle Business Journal, Encoded Therapeutics could double its Morrisville workforce as its manufacturing operations expand. The company has not yet announced a confirmed number of new local positions.

What Could This Mean for the Triangle Housing Market?

The potential expansion is another example of the Triangle's growing biotechnology and life sciences industry.

Additional specialized jobs could support housing demand in Morrisville and nearby communities, including Cary, Apex, and Raleigh.

For the local real estate market, developments like this are worth watching for several reasons:

  • Housing demand: New employees relocating to the area may increase demand for both rental properties and homes for sale.
  • Rental market: Expanding employment opportunities could benefit landlords, particularly in areas with convenient access to major employment centers.
  • Long-term growth: Continued biotechnology investment strengthens the Triangle's position as a life sciences hub.

However, the impact on housing will depend on how many jobs are actually created, hiring timelines, and where employees choose to live.

The bigger picture: While mortgage rates and home prices often dominate real estate discussions, local employment growth is another important factor shaping long-term housing demand.

For buyers and investors evaluating the Triangle market, tracking major employers and business expansions can provide valuable insight into where future demand may develop.
