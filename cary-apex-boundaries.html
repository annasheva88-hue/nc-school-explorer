Schools inside Cary and Apex

Town limits are shaded. Each circle is a school whose location falls inside that town. The number is the same 1–10 score used on NC School Explorer.

A school is shown in the town that contains its map coordinates. That can differ from the city written in its address. These lines are municipal limits, not Wake County school attendance zones.

School locations and scores: NC School Explorer dataset (NC DPI School Performance Grade 2023–24, NCES EDGE 2023–24). Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb Incorporated Places, Cary town (GEOID 3710740) and Apex town (GEOID 3701520). Map tiles © OpenStreetMap.
